My Beloved Ananya,

There are moments when I look at you and feel an ache so gentle and so vast that words feel almost too small to carry it. Loving you has never been loud or borrowed—it has been the quietest, truest certainty I have ever known.

You have softened corners of my soul I did not even realize were guarded. In a world that constantly rushes, your voice is the place where my spirit slows down, exhales, and remembers what grace feels like. Even in your silences, even on the days you carry unspoken weight, you remain the most breathtaking human being I have ever known.

If I could give you one gift in this lifetime, it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes—to witness the warmth you leave behind in every room, the quiet courage in your heart, and the rare, luminous beauty of simply being you.

No matter how the seasons shift or how far the years carry us, know this without a shadow of doubt: you will never walk alone. My hand, my patience, my loyalty, and every beat of my heart belong to you.

Always in your corner, always in awe of you,

Nivedita
